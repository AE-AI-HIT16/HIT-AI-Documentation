# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers about the platform.

## General Questions

### What is this platform?

This is a documentation platform built with Docusaurus, covering topics in AI, Frontend, DevOps, and Software Engineering.

### How do I get started?

Check out the [Getting Started](/blog/getting-started) guide for step-by-step instructions.

### Is there a free tier?

Yes, we offer a free tier with basic features. Check our pricing page for details.

## Technical Questions

### What programming languages are supported?

The platform supports multiple languages including JavaScript, Python, Go, and more.

### How do I handle errors?

Refer to the error handling section in the relevant documentation. Each API endpoint includes error response formats.

### Can I use webhooks?

Yes, webhooks are fully supported. See the webhooks documentation for setup instructions.

## Account Questions

### How do I reset my password?

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset your password.

### Can I change my account email?

Yes, you can update your email in the account settings.

### How do I delete my account?

Contact support to request account deletion.

## Billing Questions

### What payment methods are accepted?

We accept credit cards and PayPal.

### Can I get a refund?

Refund policies vary by plan. Contact support for specific refund requests.

### How do I update my billing information?

Update your billing information in the account settings under Billing.
